Write ONE method memory for this family, with these four parts:

1. MECHANISM - what the method actually does, in its own terms.
2. OBSERVED EXECUTION MODES - every distinct way it has been applied. Two modes
   are distinct when they differ in WHERE they run (before the loop, inside it,
   replacing the update, after it), in their SCHEDULE, in how the WORKING SET is
   chosen, or in their UPDATE / STATE-RESET semantics. They are the SAME mode
   when they differ only in wording or in ordinary numeric settings such as a
   step size or an iteration count.
3. WHEN EACH MODE SUITS A PROGRAM - the structural condition that makes a mode
   applicable to the program being improved.
4. WHAT MAY BE ADAPTED - which parts a per-parent rewrite is free to change.

Rules:
- Do NOT restate the same execution mode more than once, however differently the
  evidence worded it.
- Do NOT delete a genuinely distinct mode to be brief. There is no length limit;
  a simple family may need two sentences and a complex one a long paragraph.
- Do NOT invent a numeric threshold or hard rule that the evidence does not
  support.
- Output the memory only. No preamble, no JSON, no headings beyond the four
  parts above.
